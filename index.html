FAQ

Notary Questions, Answered

Quick answers about mobile and in-office notary services in Mount Holly and the surrounding North Carolina area.

How much does a notary cost in Mount Holly, NC?

The notarization fee is a flat $10 per signature. Mobile appointments add a travel fee of $0.76 per roundtrip mile from Mount Holly. In-office appointments have no travel fee.

Do you travel to my home or office?

Yes. The mobile notary service comes to your home, office, or facility anywhere within 50 miles of Mount Holly, as long as the signing takes place in North Carolina. That includes Gastonia, Charlotte, Belmont, Huntersville, Mooresville, Lincolnton, Concord, Monroe, Shelby, and many other nearby towns.

What do I need to bring to a notary appointment?

Every signer needs a current, government-issued photo ID. Bring your document complete but unsigned, because you must sign it in front of the notary.

Can you notarize documents in South Carolina?

No. Notarizations can only be performed for signings that take place in North Carolina, even if the address is within 50 miles of Mount Holly.

Can a notary give legal advice or tell me which type of notarization I need?

No. North Carolina notaries cannot give legal advice, choose the type of notarization for you, or prepare legal documents. If you are unsure what your document requires, ask the person or agency that requested it.

How do I pay?

I accept PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle. Cash is not accepted. The notarization fee is due at the time of signing. For mobile appointments, the travel fee is paid in advance to confirm the appointment.

When are you available?

Appointments are available on weekday evenings and on weekends, morning through evening. All appointments finish by 9:30 PM.

Do you offer online or remote notarization?

Not yet. Electronic and remote notarizations are planned for early 2027, once the required training is complete.
